PlanLife Modular Notion Widgets

Every aspect of the PlanLife workspace has been decoupled into individual, standalone micro-widgets. Choose your theme, copy the embed link, and paste directly into any Notion page. All widgets share data and stay synchronized in real time!

1

Choose Notion Theme

Select Notion Dark (recommended for dark workspaces), Notion Light, or Transparent.

2

Copy Embed Link

Click "Copy Notion URL" on any widget below. The link is automatically configured.

3

Paste in Notion

In your Notion page, type /embed, press Enter, paste the URL, and resize cleanly!
